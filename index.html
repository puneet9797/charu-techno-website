Refinement Steps

Edible Oil Refining Process

Continuous Degumming

Initial Purification

Designed to remove phospholipids (gums), mucilage, and trace metals from crude oil. Degumming improves the efficiency of subsequent bleaching and deodorizing stages to yield a highly stable final oil.

Key Process Steps:

1
Pre-Heating & Conditioning

Crude oil is heated to 60-80°C depending on oil type to reduce viscosity and ensure proper conditioning.

2
Acid Injection & Mixing

Phosphoric or citric acid is injected to complex trace metals and convert non-hydratable gums into hydratable forms.

3
Centrifugal Separation

A high-speed centrifugal separator discharges clear degummed oil (light phase) and hydrated gums (heavy phase).

Industrial Neutralizer

A critical component in the refining process, the Neutralizer removes acidic impurities and free fatty acids (FFA) from crude oils using a controlled caustic soda (NaOH) mixing reaction, featuring custom vertical agitators for thorough blending and soap stock separation.

Material SS304 / SS316 / MS
Capacity Up to 5000 TPD

Continuous Bleaching System
